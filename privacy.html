Site information

Privacy

How this portfolio handles information shared through its contact route.

Website browsing

This is a static portfolio hosted on GitHub Pages. This version does not intentionally add advertising pixels or a separate visitor-profiling analytics service. The site loads web fonts from Google Fonts, and the hosting provider may process standard technical request information as part of delivering the website.

Professional enquiry form

The “Submit an enquiry” link opens a Google Form in a new tab. Information you choose to submit—such as your name, email address, organisation, enquiry type, subject and message—is collected through that form, stored in its connected response spreadsheet and used to review and respond to your enquiry. Email notifications are sent to the portfolio owner.

The form is operated by Google and is subject to Google's own privacy terms and policies. Do not submit passwords, financial account details, confidential client information or other sensitive information through the form.

External links

This website links to external services, including LinkedIn, GitHub and Google Forms. Their privacy practices are governed by their respective providers.

Private certification records

Original certificate PDFs are kept in a private repository and are not embedded or linked from this public portfolio. The public site displays selected credential names and descriptions only.

Contact

For questions about information submitted through the portfolio enquiry form, contact mirshan.m@outlook.com.
