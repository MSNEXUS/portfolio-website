ProSite

A construction site tracking platform concept exploring daily logs, attendance, materials, tasks, budgets and project visibility for small and growing construction businesses.

Product concept / MVP planningMohammed Mirshan · Selected work

The problem being explored

Construction teams often coordinate site updates, workforce attendance, material movement, tasks and project costs across separate messages, spreadsheets and paper records. ProSite explores a more structured way to bring these site-level updates together.

Proposed MVP scope

  • Project dashboard and site overview.
  • Daily site logs and progress updates.
  • Worker attendance records.
  • Material tracking and basic inventory visibility.
  • Task allocation and follow-up.
  • Budget and expense tracking concepts.

Additional product ideas

Potential future integrations include location tagging, WhatsApp-based updates, downloadable reports and project analytics. These are planned ideas, not claims of completed integrations.

Approach

The work explores user needs, MVP boundaries, workflow design, possible technology choices and a phased product roadmap. The intended audience is small and medium-sized construction businesses that need clearer site-level information.

Current status

ProSite remains a product concept and MVP-planning effort. This page presents the product direction and proposed features, not a live commercial platform.
