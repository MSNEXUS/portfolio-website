Concept direction

Areas explored

  • Brand identity and positioning.
  • Curated destination and travel experiences.
  • Customer journey and service design.
  • Longer-term platform possibilities.

Current status

YolAster is a business concept, not a claim of an operating travel agency or a live booking platform.
