Business systems · Construction office management

Project COMS

An evolving Excel-based Construction Office Management System concept exploring financial tracking, employee records, payroll, documentation and operational reporting for a construction office.

Concept / systems design in progressMohammed Mirshan · Selected work

Context

Construction offices coordinate financial records, people, documents, site activity and day-to-day decisions across multiple projects. Project COMS explores how those activities could be organised through a structured office-management workflow.

What the concept covers

  • Employee and client records.
  • Income, expenses, petty cash and fine tracking.
  • Task management and operational follow-up.
  • Bills, receipts and document tracking.
  • Payroll-related records and management reporting.
  • Multi-site coordination using a central office view and site-level records.

Approach

The concept is being structured around a central office workbook supported by separate site workbooks. The intended direction includes standardised data capture, clear audit trails, summary reporting and carefully controlled automation.

The design work focuses on how an office user can move from individual transactions and records to a useful overview of projects, cash movements and outstanding tasks.

Tools and areas of learning

Microsoft Excel, workbook architecture, data validation, formulas, dashboards, VBA automation planning and process documentation.

Current status

Project COMS is an evolving concept and implementation effort. The page describes the intended system scope; it does not claim that every module is already built or deployed.
